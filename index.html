Prism 0.2.0-beta.1 · test build

One long video in. Many Shorts out.

Prism finds the strongest moments in a podcast, lecture or stream, cuts them on whole sentences, frames the speaker and adds captions. Every Short is a 1080 × 1920 MP4 you review before export.

You need a Gemini API key. Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Google bills your account, and audio, transcripts and frames are sent to Google.

macOS: this build is not notarized. The first time, right-click Prism.app and choose Open. If macOS still refuses, run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Prism.app.
